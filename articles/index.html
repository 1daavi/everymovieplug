About Every Movie Plug Articles

Articles are the longer work on Every Movie Plug: the pieces that do not fit in a headline. We write timelines that put a complicated film or franchise in order, explainers that unpack an ending or a twist, breakdowns of how directors and crews achieved a particular effect, and analysis of what box office numbers and industry decisions mean. This archive currently holds 34 articles across 3 pages, with the newest first.

What you will find here

  • Timelines and explainers. Chronologies for sprawling franchises and plain-English explanations of films with tangled plots.
  • Craft and production. Sound design, set building, camerawork and the practical problems behind famous scenes.
  • Industry and box office analysis. Why a studio strategy worked or failed, and how the biggest films compare.
  • Film history and legacy. How landmark films and series changed what came after them.
  • Previews and lists. The releases worth watching next and rankings built around a clear question.

How we write articles

Each article carries a named author and a publication date. We explain our reasoning and show the facts it rests on, and we keep opinion clearly separate from reporting: breaking news belongs in News, while articles give you analysis and context. When facts change, we update the article instead of leaving it out of date. Mistakes can be reported to contact@everymovieplug.com. Read more on our About page.

How to use this page

Articles are shown 16 to a page, newest first. Use the numbered links beneath the articles to browse older pieces, or use the search box to search every article at once. The digest below the list tells you roughly how long each article on the page takes to read.

Frequently asked questions

How often do you publish new articles?

New articles are added to page 1 as they are published. Older ones move back one place at a time and stay in the archive.

Are the articles spoiler-free?

Not always. Breakdowns and endings explained discuss plot details, so check the headline and opening paragraph before you read a film you have not yet seen.

Can I suggest a topic?

Yes. Email contact@everymovieplug.com with the film, series or question you would like us to cover.
