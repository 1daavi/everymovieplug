A Complete Breakdown of the Tenet Timeline

Unscrambling the mechanics, turnstiles, and loops of cinema’s most complex puzzle.

Christopher Nolan’s 2020 sci-fi thriller Tenet presents one of the most intricate timeline puzzles in cinematic history. Unlike classic time-travel films where characters teleport instantaneously between eras, Tenet operates under the law of inversion: entropy is reversed, forcing individuals to live backward through time at normal speed to reach the past.

What appears on screen as a linear sequence of events is actually a complex, overlapping causal loop: a closed structure where cause and effect seamlessly inform each other, forming a cinematic palindrome.

Below is a complete, step-by-step breakdown of how the timeline, turnstiles, and temporal pincer movements of Tenet fit together.

1. The Mechanics: How Time Works in Tenet

To understand the loop, you must first understand three core concepts:

  • Inversion & Turnstiles: A Turnstile is a machine that reverses a person's or object's entropy. Entering a turnstile does not send you to 1995 instantly; it flips your arrow of time. If you stay inverted for two hours, you travel two hours into the past.
  • Temporal Pincer Movement: A military tactic where one team moves forward through time (Red Team) and another team moves backward through time (Blue Team). Blue Team experiences the outcome first, then inverts to feed information back to Red Team before the mission begins.
  • "What’s Happened, Happened": The universe in Tenet is deterministic. You cannot change the past; you can only fulfill the role you were always meant to play.

2. Chronological Breakdown of the Film’s Major Events

While the story is presented from the perspective of The Protagonist (John David Washington), the true timeline spans across years. Here is the overarching narrative in chronological sequence:

Step 1: The Distant Future & The Creation of the Algorithm

Decades into the future, a brilliant scientist creates The Algorithm: a device capable of permanently reversing the entropy of the entire planet. Realizing that inverting the Earth will destroy all past generations to save the future's ruined environment, she divides the Algorithm into 9 physical pieces, inverts them, and hides them in the past inside secure nuclear storage facilities.

She then takes her own life to ensure the secret dies with her.

Step 2: Sator’s Origin (Stalsk-12)

Years before the main events of the film, a young Andrei Sator (Kenneth Branagh) is in his irradiated hometown of Stalsk-12 during the Soviet Union's collapse. While searching for weaponized plutonium, he unearths a message and inverted gold sent from the future.

The future faction recruits Sator to gather the 9 hidden pieces of the Algorithm in exchange for wealth and power.

Step 3: The Anchor Point: Day 0 (14th of the Month)

Three major events occur simultaneously on this critical day:

  1. Kyiv Opera Siege: The Protagonist (working for the CIA) attempts to retrieve a piece of the Algorithm (disguised as Plutonium-241). He is saved by a masked soldier wearing a red string pull-tab on his backpack (Neil).
  2. Stalsk-12 Detonation: Sator’s men detonate a bomb in Stalsk-12 to bury the assembled Algorithm in a deep underground hypocenter.
  3. The Vietnam Yacht Incident: Sator spends what he considers his last happy moment with his wife Kat (Elizabeth Debicki) on their yacht off the coast of Vietnam. Sator disappears, and Kat sees a mystery woman jump off the boat into the sea.

Step 4: The Forward Journey (The Protagonist’s Awakening)

After surviving a fake cyanide pill, The Protagonist is inducted into TENET.

  • Mumbai: He meets Neil (Robert Pattinson), who already knows his habits and beverage preferences. Together, they contact arms dealer Priya.
  • Oslo Freeport: To get closer to Sator, they attempt to destroy a fake Goya painting stored in a secure Oslo vault. During the raid, they crash a cargo plane and discover a Turnstile. The Protagonist fights a mysterious, masked inverted soldier coming out of the machine.
  • Tallinn Highway Chase: In Estonia, the Protagonist secures the final piece of the Algorithm. However, an inverted Sator initiates a temporal pincer movement, takes Kat hostage, shoots her with an inverted bullet, and escapes with the final piece.

Step 5: Inverting Backward Through Time

To heal Kat’s inverted wound, The Protagonist, Neil, and Kat invert themselves through Sator’s Tallinn turnstile, stepping backward into time:

  1. The Reversed Highway Chase: The Protagonist pursues Sator backward down the Tallinn highway, inadvertently delivering the Algorithm piece right into Sator's hands (a classic closed-loop paradox).
  2. Return to Oslo (1 Week Prior): Sealed inside a shipping container, they travel back to the day of the Oslo plane crash. The Protagonist fights his past self (revealing he was the masked soldier from earlier) before entering the Turnstile to revert back to moving forward in time.

Step 6: The Climax at Day 0 (The Final Temporal Pincer)

Once Kat is healed, the team realizes Sator, dying of terminal pancreatic cancer, plans to return to the yacht in Vietnam on Day 0. Sator has linked a fitness tracker (a dead man’s switch) to the Algorithm. If his heart stops, an automated email will broadcast the location of the buried Algorithm to the future, triggering global annihilation.

3. The Ultimate Twist: Neil’s Loop & TENET’s Founder

During the climax at Stalsk-12, The Protagonist and Ives are trapped behind a locked gate inside the hypocenter, with a dead soldier wearing a red string pull-tab on his mask lying on the other side.

As an inverted henchman prepares to shoot the Protagonist:

  1. The dead soldier suddenly "un-dies" (from a forward perspective), rises, blocks the bullet, unlocks the gate, runs out, and disappears.
  2. The Protagonist and Ives secure the Algorithm.

After the mission, Neil reveals he was that soldier. To make the mission succeed, Neil must invert again, travel back into the underground bunker, unlock the gate, and take the bullet for the Protagonist.

"I've known you for years. You have a future in the past. It’s an end for me, Protagonist... but for you, it’s just the beginning." — Neil

The Protagonist realizes he himself is the mastermind who founded TENET in the future. Future-Protagonist recruits a younger Neil, trains him, and sends him backward through time to guide his younger self through the events of the movie.

Final Thoughts: The Perfect Palindrome

Tenet is structured like its namesake: a word that reads the same backward and forward. Every action taken by the characters to save the world was already built into the history of that world. There are no alternative realities or split timelines in Tenet; there is only one immutable thread of time woven by those brave enough to walk backward through it.
