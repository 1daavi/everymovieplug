What Biswajit Das covers

Film and TV news. Casting announcements, release dates, box office results, trailers, streaming premieres and renewals, each written up with the original reporting linked at the bottom of the story so readers can check it for themselves.

In-depth articles. Longer pieces that look at how films are made and why they work: timelines and explainers, the engineering and craft behind famous sets and formats, and analysis of directors, franchises and box office history.

Scene breakdowns and movie history. Iconic sequences and landmark films, with context for viewers who want more than a trailer. Every Movie Plug is also on X: follow @everymovieplug and join the community, where film fans from around the world talk about what they are watching.
