About Every Movie Plug News

Every Movie Plug News is where we report on what is happening in film and television: weekend box office results, release-date changes, streaming premieres, casting announcements, renewals and cancellations, trailers, and the business moves behind the projects people are waiting for. Each story starts with the facts — what happened, who is involved and when you can watch it — and then adds the context that explains why it matters. This archive currently holds 134 stories across 9 pages, with the newest first.

What we cover

  • Box office. Opening weekends, second-weekend drops, running domestic totals and how a result compares with the studio’s expectations.
  • Streaming and TV. Premiere dates, season renewals, episode schedules, finales and what is arriving on the major services.
  • Casting and development. Who has joined a project, which films and series are moving into production, and which have been delayed or paused.
  • Franchises, games and comics. Adaptations of video games and comics, sequels, spin-offs and the shared universes that connect them.
  • Industry and awards. Studio mergers, festival and awards-season news, and the people and companies shaping what reaches screens.

How we report

News stories name the outlets they draw on and list them at the bottom of the page, so you can read the original reporting for yourself. When reports conflict, we say so instead of picking a side. When a story develops, we update the original page with the latest facts and mark it “Updated” rather than publishing near-duplicates. If you spot a mistake, email contact@everymovieplug.com and we will review it and fix it. We are not paid to feature a title. Analysis and opinion live in our Articles section, and our full editorial standards are on the About page.

How to use this page

Stories are shown 16 to a page, newest first. Use the numbered links under the stories to move back through older coverage, or type a name, title or topic into the search box at the top to search every story on the site at once — not only the page you are on. Each tile shows its category and publication date, so you can tell at a glance whether it is a box office report, a streaming update or a casting scoop.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find older news?

Use the page numbers beneath the stories. Every story we have published stays available in this archive, and the search box looks through all of them.

What is the difference between News and Articles?

News reports what has just happened. Articles are longer pieces — timelines, explainers, craft breakdowns and film history — that are written to stay useful for longer.

Where do you get your movie and TV information?

Stories cite their sources. Film and TV data elsewhere on the site, such as cast lists and ratings, comes from TMDB, and streaming availability comes from JustWatch.

How do I report an error or suggest a story?

Email contact@everymovieplug.com with a link to the story and what you think should change.
