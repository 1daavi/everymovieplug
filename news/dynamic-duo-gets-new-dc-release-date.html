DC Sets a New Release Date for Dynamic Duo

The animated film about Dick Grayson and Jason Todd will arrive in September 2028 as a DC Elseworlds project.

DC Studios has set a new release date for Dynamic Duo, moving the animated film to September 22, 2028. The project, produced under James Gunn and Peter Safran’s DC Studios banner, is part of the studio’s separate Elseworlds label rather than the main DC Universe continuity.

The film had previously been scheduled for June 30, 2028. The revised date places it later in the same year and gives the project a distinct place on DC’s expanding slate of theatrical releases. The story focuses on Dick Grayson and Jason Todd, two characters who become different versions of Robin in Batman’s world.

A different kind of Robin story

Dynamic Duo is being developed as a hybrid production combining puppetry, computer-generated imagery, and other visual techniques. Rather than presenting a conventional live-action superhero adventure, the film is designed to give the two young heroes a visual identity of its own.

The project is classified as an Elseworlds film, a label DC uses for stories that exist outside the central continuity of its shared universe. That distinction matters as the studio develops interconnected DCU stories alongside standalone interpretations of its characters.

Where the film fits into DC’s plans

Gunn and Safran have repeatedly described a strategy that allows some projects to contribute to a larger narrative while letting others work as independent stories. The Elseworlds label gives filmmakers room to explore familiar characters without having to fit every plot point into the same timeline as the main DCU.

The main DCU’s announced theatrical slate includes Superman: Man of Tomorrow, scheduled for July 9, 2027. By comparison, Dynamic Duo remains a separate project, so its release should not be read as confirmation of a new chapter or crossover in the core DCU storyline.

With the new date now set for September 2028, audiences have a clearer window for the animated project, although further details about its voice cast, story, and marketing plans will depend on future announcements from DC Studios.
