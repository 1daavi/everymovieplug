Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: Mutant Mayhem 2 Moves Up to August 13, 2027

By Maccarthy Albin

October 19, 2026

The animated sequel arrives about a month earlier than planned, with a separate live-action hybrid film already dated for 2028.

Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: Mutant Mayhem 2 now arrives in theaters on August 13, 2027. Paramount and Point Grey moved the sequel up from its earlier September 17, 2027 date, according to 3DVF.

Jeff Rowe, who directed the 2023 original, is attached to the sequel. Micah Abbey, Shamon Brown Jr., Nicolas Cantu and Brady Noon are listed as returning voices for the four brothers, and Ayo Edebiri is expected back as April O’Neil. Rowe has described the follow-up as a villain-forward film, per Screen Rant.

Paramount has also dated a separate, untitled live-action/CG hybrid Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles film for November 17, 2028. Variety reports that Neal H. Moritz, the producer behind the Sonic the Hedgehog movies, is producing it, and that it would be the first live-action Turtles film since 2016’s Out of the Shadows.

Plot details for both films have not been announced. Paramount has not said how the animated sequel and the live-action reboot will relate to each other.
