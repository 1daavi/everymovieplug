Upcoming Stephen King Movies and Shows After Carrie

Only a few projects have dates. Most of the next wave is still in development.

Mike Flanagan's Carrie is now streaming, and the next Stephen King adaptations are mostly still undated. The Institute season 2 is the next one with a firm date, November 8, according to GamesRadar's October 7 update.

Already out: All eight episodes of Carrie arrived on Prime Video on October 7, per The Popverse. Flanagan developed the series, which stars Summer H. Howell as Carrie White and Samantha Sloyan as Margaret White, with Matthew Lillard, Kate Siegel, Katee Sackhoff and Heather Graham among the cast.

TV with dates or renewals: The Institute season 2 premieres November 8, GamesRadar reports, with Mary-Louise Parker returning and Alfie Allen joining. It: Welcome to Derry has been renewed for season 2 at HBO without a release date. A24's Fairy Tale is a 10-episode series with J.H. Wyman as showrunner and no date, and The Dark Tower is in development at Prime Video from Flanagan and Trevor Macy as five seasons plus two standalone features.

Movies in development: Flanagan is attached to The Mist. Mister Yummy is set to be directed by Ben Young, and J.T. Mollner is set to direct The Girl Who Loved Tom Gordon, with Lionsgate holding the rights. Netflix announced a Cujo remake with Roy Lee producing, and CinemaBlend lists The Stand with Doug Liman directing, announced by Paramount in June 2025. GamesRadar also lists Rat from director Isaac Ezban, with Ben Stiller possibly starring but unconfirmed.

Billy Summers is reported with Ed Zwick and Marshall Herskovitz writing. Outlets disagree on Leonardo DiCaprio: JustWatch lists him as attached, while GamesRadar calls his involvement speculation.

Lists also include projects described as stalled, such as Christine and The Tommyknockers, according to CinemaBlend.
