Janelle James to Host the 32nd Annual Critics Choice Awards

The Abbott Elementary star will lead the ceremony in Santa Monica as the awards season gets underway.

Janelle James will host the 32nd annual Critics Choice Awards, bringing the comedian and Abbott Elementary star to the stage for one of the first major ceremonies of the 2027 awards season. The Critics Choice Association announced the appointment on October 29.

The ceremony is scheduled for Sunday, January 3, 2027, at Barker Hangar in Santa Monica, California. The show will air live on E! and USA Network from 7 p.m. to 10 p.m. Eastern Time, with the broadcast carried across both coasts.

A familiar face from one of television’s biggest comedies

James is best known to many viewers as Ava Coleman, the confident and frequently unpredictable principal in ABC’s Emmy-nominated comedy Abbott Elementary. Her performance has earned her five consecutive Emmy nominations for Outstanding Supporting Actress in a Comedy Series, along with a Critics Choice win in 2026.

She has also received recognition from the NAACP Image Awards, including wins for Outstanding Supporting Actress in a Comedy Series in 2023 and 2026. Outside Abbott Elementary, her credits include Black Monday, Apple TV’s animated series Central Park, and the feature comedy One of Them Days.

James is also preparing to release her first hour-long stand-up special, Janelle James: How Romantic, on Netflix on November 10, 2026. Hosting the Critics Choice Awards gives her another prominent platform to bring her comedy to a broad film and television audience.

When will nominations be announced?

The Critics Choice Association says nominations for both film and television will be announced on Friday, December 4, 2026. The announcement is scheduled for noon Eastern Time and will be presented by Erin Lim Rhodes and Will Marfuggi of Live From E!, with the nominees also published through the awards organization’s website and social channels.

The ceremony will continue the Critics Choice Awards’ combined format, recognizing achievements across theatrical films, television, and streaming. The event is produced by Bob Bain Productions and Berlin Entertainment.

For James, the hosting role marks a return to an awards show that already recognized her work this year. The next ceremony will put her in a different position: guiding the evening while the industry celebrates its standout performances and productions of 2026.
