Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey Arrives on Digital and 4K Blu-ray November 17

Universal has confirmed the home release, with 12 behind-the-scenes featurettes overseen by Nolan.

Universal has confirmed that Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey arrives on digital and 4K Ultra HD Blu-ray on November 17, 2026, according to JoBlo. The date had been rumored for months.

The release includes a package of bonus material. Twelve behind-the-scenes featurettes, overseen by Nolan, cover the adaptation of the classic poem, the casting and costumes, filming in extreme conditions, the redesigned Trojan Horse, the Moroccan set built for the fall of Troy, and the 15th-century castle on Favignana used for Ithaca. Others look at the action work with Matt Damon and Tom Holland, the visual effects, the Viking ships and storm sequences, and Ludwig Göransson's score, which uses ancient instruments and vocals.

The discs also carry The Journey of Film on The Odyssey, in which creator Reece Feldman asks Nolan what happens to a movie after it leaves the set, plus an IMAX exclusive prologue, the music video for “When I'm Home” by James Blake, Travis Scott and Göransson, and trailers.

The home release follows a return to the big screen. The film went back to select IMAX and IMAX 70mm theaters on October 9, according to Bleeding Cool, giving fans another chance to see it in the largest formats before the home release.
