Hugh Jackman’s R-Rated Thriller The Death of Robin Hood Hits No. 1 on HBO Max After Weak Box Office

The A24 film grossed under $10 million in theaters but topped HBO Max’s US chart after one day.

Hugh Jackman’s The Death of Robin Hood is now streaming on HBO Max and reached No. 1 on the service’s US weekly chart after a single day, according to Yahoo. It is still outside the global top 10.

A24 released the R-rated film in theaters on June 19, 2026. SlashFilm reports it grossed less than $10 million, and digital rental and purchase followed in late July, per Screen Rant.

Michael Sarnoski (Pig, A Quiet Place: Day One) wrote and directed. Jackman plays an older Robin Hood reckoning with a violent past after a serious injury. Jodie Comer, Bill Skarsgård, Noah Jupe and Murray Bartlett co-star. Critics have compared Jackman’s performance to Logan.

The film holds roughly 68 to 69 percent on Rotten Tomatoes, per Screen Rant and Yahoo.
