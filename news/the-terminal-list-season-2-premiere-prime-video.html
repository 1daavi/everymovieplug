The Terminal List Season 2 Is Now Streaming on Prime Video With All Eight Episodes

By Maccarthy Albin

October 21, 2026

Chris Pratt's James Reece is offered a presidential pardon to stop a global terror threat.

The Terminal List Season 2 premieres Wednesday, October 21 on Prime Video, and all eight episodes are available at once, according to Forbes and The Week.

Chris Pratt returns as former Navy SEAL James Reece. Having finished his revenge mission, he is offered a presidential pardon by the CIA to help stop a growing global terror threat, per The Week. The story moves from Moscow to CIA headquarters in Langley, Virginia, and adapts Jack Carr's novel True Believer.

Constance Wu and Luke Hemsworth return. Tom Hopper, Dar Salim, Costa Ronin and Olga Kurylenko are among the new additions, and Forbes also lists Gabriel Luna, Yul Vazquez and Arnold Vosloo. Taylor Kitsch will not return, according to Forbes. Prime Video has not confirmed the exact U.S. release hour, though Forbes expects a midnight Pacific drop.

The season connects to the 2025 prequel The Terminal List: Dark Wolf, which follows characters including Hopper's Raife Hastings and Hemsworth's Jules Landry, The Week says. Season 1 drew a 42% Rotten Tomatoes critics score and a 94% audience score.
