The Mummy 4 Set Photos Show Brendan Fraser and Rachel Weisz Back Together

The first joint look comes as the sequel films in London, with a release set for October 2027.

New set photos show Brendan Fraser and Rachel Weisz together on The Mummy 4. The images surfaced on October 14, according to JoBlo and Comic Basics, and are the first to show the pair together on the new film.

In the new photos Weisz wears a deep red skirt suit with a belted jacket, and Fraser wears a brown jacket over a plaid shirt and jeans, according to Comic Basics. Thought Catalog's post places the sighting in West London.

The first photos came on September 28, when the New York Post shared a first look at Weisz in costume. Comic Basics reports Weisz was photographed outside London's British Museum, and that Fraser was seen driving a vintage rocket car in Suffolk, reportedly in a record-speed trials scene. Production began in August in London and Morocco, according to Comic Basics.

Directed by Matt Bettinelli-Olpin and Tyler Gillett of Radio Silence from a script by David Coggeshall, the film is a direct sequel to The Mummy Returns, according to Deadline as cited by Comic Basics. John Hannah, Oded Fehr and Kevin J. O'Connor also return, and Michael Johnston and Numan Acar are new. Plot details have not been revealed.

Universal moved the release up from May 19, 2028 to October 15, 2027, according to JoBlo.
