Netflix Renews A Different World and Crew Girl for Season 2

By Maccarthy Albin

October 10, 2026

Two Netflix series have been picked up for another round.

Netflix has renewed A Different World and Crew Girl for second seasons, TVLine reports.

The two renewals are part of a busy stretch of pickups across television this fall, as networks and streamers decide which of their newer series will return. TVLine's renewal and cancellation scorecard tracks the status of current shows.

Netflix has not yet announced premiere dates for either second season. We will update this post when dates are confirmed.

A Different World: a fast renewal

Netflix's A Different World is a revival of the classic sitcom, set once again at Hillman College. It premiered on September 24, 2026, and the renewal was reported on October 2, barely a week later. According to What's on Netflix, the series debuted at No. 4 on Netflix's Global Top 10 for English-language TV with 4.2 million views, and reached the Top 10 in 15 countries.

Felicia Pride, whose writing credits include Grey's Anatomy and Bel-Air, created the series and serves as showrunner. Debbie Allen, who was the original show's primary director from 1989 to 1993, is an executive producer and directs, which ties the new series directly to the old one. Tracey Pakosta, Netflix's Vice President of Comedy Series, said the team can't wait to see "what unfolds next year at Hillman College," which suggests Season 2 is planned for 2027.

Netflix's logline for Season 2 has the students returning for sophomore year with a "New Year, New Me" attitude, with storylines about past decisions, uncertain futures, new relationships and friendships, and questions of purpose.

Crew Girl: a rowing drama returns

Crew Girl is a Canadian drama that premiered on Netflix on September 10, 2026, with eight episodes. It follows Teagan Tao (Miku Martineau), a teenage rowing prodigy whose family loses its financial security when her hotelier father's embezzlement scheme comes to light. She moves to her mother's hometown in Massachusetts and becomes the coxswain for the boys' varsity crew team at Easton Prep.

The cast also includes Sam Braun as Josh Regis, Kyle Clark as Cam Dillinger, Thomas Cadrot as Coach Hayden, Jessica Paré as Ella Emerson-Tao and Connor Paton as Ryder Regis. Vivian Lin created the series and is its showrunner. TVLine reported the Season 2 renewal on September 22.

What we still don't know

Neither renewal comes with a premiere date, an episode count, or details about returning cast. We will update this page as Netflix confirms more. The A Different World renewal in particular shows how quickly the service will move on a new series after strong early viewing.
