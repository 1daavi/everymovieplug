The Beekeeper 2 Trailer Released: Jason Statham Returns January 15

By Maccarthy Albin

October 15, 2026

The first trailer shows a Super Bee with a minigun, a flamethrower fight and a rogue Beekeepers organization.

The first trailer for The Beekeeper 2 is out. Amazon MGM Studios released it on Thursday, October 15, and the film opens in theaters on January 15, 2027, according to Collider and ComingSoon. Jason Statham returns as Adam Clay.

What the Trailer Shows

The trailer sets up Clay as a wanted man whose former organization, the Beekeepers, has gone rogue, according to Collider. He must protect the U.S. President from his former colleagues. ComingSoon says the footage opens with Statham beside a fire as a bee lands on his fingers, and shows him driving a 1969 Dodge Super Bee fitted with a stinger. It ends with a flamethrower fight and Clay's line, “Next time, don't be so hotheaded.”

The premise reverses the situation of the first film. In the 2024 original, Clay was a former operative of a secret organization who used his skills against criminals who had targeted a vulnerable victim. In the sequel, as described by Collider, the organization he once belonged to is the threat, and he becomes the target. The bee imagery, from the insect on his hand to the car’s name and its stinger, shows that the film intends to keep the series’ beekeeping motif at the center of its visual identity.

Looking Back at the First Film

The original The Beekeeper arrived in January 2024, directed by Ayer, with Statham as Clay, a man who had left a covert organization and was living a quiet life tending bees. The story turned violent when a person close to him was harmed by a scam, and Clay used his old skills to pursue those responsible. That film established the premise, the tone and the beekeeping imagery that the sequel’s trailer now builds on. Viewers who have not seen it may find it useful to watch before the sequel opens, since the new story depends on the history of the Beekeepers and on characters introduced earlier.

A New Director Takes Over

Timo Tjahjanto, who directed Nobody 2, replaces David Ayer as director. Jeremy Irons returns as Wallace Westwyld, and Emmy Raver-Lampman, Bobby Naderi and Jemma Redgrave also reprise their roles. New cast members include Pom Klementieff, Yara Shahidi and Adam Copeland.

Tjahjanto is an Indonesian filmmaker whose earlier work includes The Night Comes for Us, a martial arts film known for its intense violence. His most recent credit, Nobody 2, also falls in the action genre, which makes him a natural fit for a franchise built around a lead who dispatches opponents in elaborate fashion. The change from Ayer, who directed the first film, means the sequel will have a different visual hand behind it, though the principal star and several of the original supporting players remain in place.

The returning cast also provides continuity. Irons’s Westwyld was a figure from the Beekeepers’ world in the first film, and the presence of Raver-Lampman, Naderi and Redgrave connects the new story to characters audiences have already met.

The Newcomers

Klementieff, Shahidi and Copeland come from different corners of film, television and wrestling. Klementieff is known for her work in major franchise films, Shahidi for television, and Copeland for a long career in professional wrestling before moving into acting. The trailer coverage cited here does not describe the roles they play, so their characters remain unclear until the studio releases more material.

Release Strategy and Timing

Amazon MGM Studios handles the theatrical release, and Dark Horizons reports that Miramax finances and produces the film. It opens on the Martin Luther King Jr. holiday weekend, three years after the 2024 original.

A mid-January release has become familiar territory for action titles, since the weekend falls after the crowded holiday season and gives a film room to attract an audience before the larger spring releases arrive. The three-year gap between the original and its sequel is also notable. It places the sequel at a point when audiences have had time to look forward to another installment, with a new director and an expanded cast attached.

The theatrical release is presented as an Amazon MGM Studios project, while the financing and production credit with Miramax reflects the two companies’ involvement in the film behind the scenes. Both details come from the cited reports and are not expanded on further in the sources.

What to Take Away From the First Look

Trailers present only a selected slice of a film, and this one is no exception. Based on the coverage, viewers can expect a story that returns Statham to the role of a lone figure being pursued by his former allies, with a new director and spectacle on display in the armed Super Bee and the flamethrower sequence. Details about the plot beyond the President’s protection, the identity of the main antagonist and the full roles of the new cast members are not revealed.

With the January 15, 2027 opening date confirmed, the next stretch of the marketing campaign is likely to include additional trailers, posters and promotional appearances. For now, the first trailer offers a clear view of the tone: action-driven, centered on its lead, and built around the idea that the most dangerous opponent may be the organization that trained him.
