Resident Evil Tops $127 Million as Holdovers Stay Strong

Several recent releases are holding steady behind newcomer Verity.

Behind the new releases, several films from recent weeks continue to build their totals at the domestic box office.

Where They Stand

  • Resident Evil: $12.5 million over the October 2 weekend, down 46%. It reached about $127.9 million after 19 days in release.
  • Heart of the Beast: $11.0 million for the weekend, down 45%, with about $40.8 million total after 12 days.
  • Primetime: $8.0 million for the weekend, down 58%, bringing its total to about $34.1 million after 10 days.
  • Forgotten Island: $7.2 million for the weekend, down 45%, with about $24.9 million total after 12 days.

Resident Evil remains the clear leader of the group, and it is the only one of the four past $100 million domestically.
