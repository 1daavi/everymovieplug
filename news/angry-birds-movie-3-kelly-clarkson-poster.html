The Angry Birds Movie 3 Pokes Fun at the Kelly Clarkson Mechanical Bird Feud in New Poster

By Maccarthy Albin

October 17, 2026

The animated poster casts Chuck as the wall-mounted bird at the center of the viral dispute.

The Angry Birds Movie 3 has joined the Kelly Clarkson feud. The film's official X account posted a parody poster that casts Chuck as Molly, the mechanical bird at the center of Clarkson's dispute with a Brooklyn store, according to ComingSoon and Creative Bloq. The film opens in theaters on December 23.

The Joke on the Poster

The poster jokes that you can see Chuck for the price of a movie ticket, a dig at the bird's price tag. Its tagline reads “What Doesn't Kill You Makes You…Angry,” according to ComingSoon. Creative Bloq dates the embedded post to October 15 and says the animated version shows Chuck sitting back while others get angry.

The gag works on two levels. The yellow-feathered Chuck is the fast, hyperactive member of the flock, so casting him as a motionless wall ornament is a reversal that fans of the series can appreciate on its own. The reference to price then ties the joke to the news story, and the tagline plays on a familiar pop lyric associated with Clarkson, though the sources do not say whether that echo was intended.

How the Dispute Unfolded

The feud began on Saturday, October 10, when Clarkson posted on X that staff at Art of Play, a Brooklyn Heights shop, treated her as if she could not afford an item and steered her to cheaper alternatives, according to Forbes. The store said the bird, Molly, is permanently attached to the wall for its magic shows and was never for sale. Clarkson replied that a price tag was near the bird and that an employee quoted her $15,000.

On Monday, October 12, the store said it had offered her a nearly identical bird by Seattle artist Bliss Kolb for $15,000, plus a different mechanical bird for $2,000, per Forbes. It said it had reached out to Clarkson privately. Forbes reports her two posts have drawn about 50 million views.

The two accounts contradict each other on key points, and the sources cited here do not settle which version is accurate. Clarkson, the singer and television host who first rose to fame as the first winner of American Idol in 2002, describes a price quote on an object she believed was for sale. The store says the object was never for sale and that it later offered alternatives. Because the details rest on competing statements, this report presents each side as it was described and draws no conclusion.

Why Studios Jump on Viral Moments

Brands and studios regularly respond to trending stories with jokes and parodies, a practice often called real-time marketing. For a film heading toward a holiday release, a topical poster can reach audiences that a standard promotional image would not. The cost is low, the turnaround is fast, and a well-received joke gets shared by users who may not otherwise engage with an animated sequel.

There is some risk in attaching a film to a live dispute, since the subject is a real person and a real business and public sentiment can change. The poster keeps its target light by focusing on the bird and its price rather than on either party’s conduct, which allows the film to participate without taking a position. Neither Clarkson nor the store has been quoted in the cited coverage as reacting to the poster.

The Film Behind the Poster

Directed by John Rice from a screenplay by Thurop Van Orman, the film has a 96-minute runtime, according to ComingSoon. Jason Sudeikis returns as Red, with Josh Gad as Chuck, Rachel Bloom as Silver and Danny McBride as Bomb. New voices include Emma Myers, Walker Scobell, Tim Robinson and Psalm West, and MrBeast, Keke Palmer, Lily James and Nikki Glaser are among those in undisclosed roles. Animation Magazine says the story follows Red “surviving fatherhood while saving the world.”

The series began with The Angry Birds Movie in 2016 and continued with a sequel in 2019, both based on the mobile game from Finnish developer Rovio. The new film brings back the main flock and adds a large group of fresh voices, including internet personalities and comedians alongside actors. The premise of a lead character juggling parenthood and a world-threatening problem gives the franchise a more domestic angle than its earlier adventures, though plot details beyond that line have not been shared.

A Franchise With a Long Memory

Angry Birds first appeared as a mobile game from Rovio in 2009, and the brand has since expanded into films, television shows and merchandise. Rovio was acquired by Sega in 2023, which adds a further layer to the franchise’s long history of changing hands and formats. A topical joke from the film’s official account is one more way the brand keeps its characters in front of an audience that has known them for well over a decade.

The poster is also a reminder that the franchise targets two audiences at once: children who watch the films and adults who grew up playing the game. A joke about the price of a mechanical bird is aimed squarely at the second group, while the poster’s bright animation keeps it accessible to the first.

Release Timeline

Paramount released the trailer on June 29. The release was moved up from January 29, 2027 to December 23, 2026, according to Wikipedia.

Moving a film earlier is less common than delaying one, and a date of December 23 places it in the heart of the holiday season, when family audiences have time off school and theaters see heavy attendance. The shift also brings the film into a busy window with other holiday releases, a competitive environment that makes visibility important. Seen in that light, the topical poster is part of a push to keep the film in public view as the date approaches.

What to Watch

The next updates are likely to be additional marketing material, character posters and further details about the undisclosed roles. Whether the dispute continues to produce news may also affect how long the parody stays in conversation. For now, the confirmed facts are the poster, the release date of December 23 and the cast and crew listed above.
