VisionQuest Episodes 3 and 4 Are Streaming Now on Disney+: Release Schedule and What’s Next

Spoilers for episodes 3 and 4 below. Episodes 5 and 6 arrive next week.

VisionQuest episodes 3 and 4, ‘Reboot’ and ‘Malware,’ are now streaming on Disney+. In the US they arrived Tuesday, October 20 at 9 p.m. ET, per The Direct. Internationally they arrive the next day.

Spoilers follow. Screen Rant says the episodes introduce Jocasta, played by T’Nia Miller, who is revealed as Dr. Josephine Carrington, one of the minds behind early AI. The episodes also explore the backstory of Paladin (Todd Stashwick) and show Tommy (Ruaridh Mollica) helping Vision in a life-threatening moment. Ultron, voiced by James Spader, interacts with Jocasta at the end of episode 4 while controlling Vision’s body. Paul Bettany stars as Vision.

Marvel changed the schedule two days before launch. In the US, new episodes now arrive on Tuesdays instead of Wednesdays, per Nerdist. Episodes 5 and 6 arrive October 27, episode 7 on November 3 and the finale on November 10. International dates fall one day later.

The eight-episode series follows WandaVision and Agatha All Along. It is the final Marvel TV series of 2026, according to TechRadar. Disney+’s own page still lists Wednesday dates, so check your local schedule.
