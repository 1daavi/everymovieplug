Dune: Part Three Popcorn Buckets Bring Back the Sandworms

The new theater collectibles include a double-sandworm bucket, a thumper design, and a Spacing Guild Navigator version.

The collectible popcorn buckets for Dune: Part Three are leaning into the sandworm spectacle once again, with AMC Theatres unveiling a double-sandworm design alongside a second version built around a single worm and a thumper. The new merchandise was revealed on October 29, ahead of the film’s December theatrical release.

The double-sandworm bucket features two openings shaped like the creatures’ mouths, giving moviegoers a design intended for sharing. The alternate AMC collectible features one sandworm and a thumper, the device used in the Dune universe to create rhythmic vibrations that attract sandworms across the desert.

AMC is not the only theater chain with a collectible

The new designs follow the unexpected popularity of the sandworm-shaped bucket released for Dune: Part Two in 2024. That earlier collectible became a viral talking point, drawing jokes and parodies while helping turn elaborate movie-theater containers into a marketing event of their own.

AMC says the latest items were designed to meet the high expectations created by the previous release. Other theater chains are also preparing their own merchandise. Cinemark has previewed a Copper Sandworm bucket, while Regal has revealed a Spacing Guild Navigator design inspired by the film’s futuristic travel technology.

When can fans get the new buckets?

AMC has announced December 17 as the date for its exclusive collectibles, one day before Dune: Part Three opens in theaters on December 18, 2026. Other chains have also indicated December availability, but exact dates, prices, and stock levels may vary by retailer and location.

The film is directed by Denis Villeneuve and continues the story of Paul Atreides after the events of Dune: Part Two. Timothée Chalamet returns as Paul, with Zendaya and other returning cast members reprising their roles. The third film is expected to adapt elements of Frank Herbert’s novel Dune Messiah.

For theaters, the merchandise offers another way to turn a major release into an event beyond the screen. For fans, the question is whether the new sandworm designs can match the internet-wide attention generated by the previous bucket.
