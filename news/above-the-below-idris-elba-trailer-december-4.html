Above the Below Trailer: Idris Elba’s Astronaut Survival Thriller Opens December 4

By Maccarthy Albin

October 21, 2026

Three astronauts survive a violent re-entry, only to find their capsule sinking into the ocean.

Lionsgate has released the first trailer for Above the Below, a survival thriller co-directed by and starring Idris Elba. The film opens in theaters December 4, 2026, per Dark Horizons. Rotten Tomatoes lists it as a limited release.

The trailer shows three astronauts surviving a destructive re-entry and crash-landing in the ocean, where their capsule begins to sink with oxygen running low. They must decide who will risk reaching the surface and who stays behind with the wreckage, according to Lionsgate’s synopsis.

What the Trailer Reveals

The footage is built around a simple and unforgiving idea: the danger does not end when a spacecraft returns to Earth. After a violent descent, the crew finds that the ocean they landed in is a threat of its own. Communications with the outside world are cut, the cabin is taking on water, and the air inside is running out. The marketing leans on confinement, showing tight interiors, flickering emergency lighting and characters forced to make decisions with almost no time to weigh them.

Images released alongside the trailer also show crew members in diving masks moving through dark open water, which suggests the story will leave the capsule and play out underwater as well as inside the wreck. The shift from the vacuum of space to the pressure of the deep sea gives the film a hook that sets it apart from most space survival stories, which typically keep their characters in orbit or on a distant surface.

The Cast and Their Characters

Elba plays Jackson, the crew member at the center of the story. Caitlin FitzGerald, known for her work on the HBO drama Succession, plays Brooks, and Hero Fiennes Tiffin plays Rhodes, per FirstShowing. With only three principal characters, the film depends heavily on how convincingly the actors convey fear, exhaustion and mistrust as the situation worsens. A cast this small also means that every choice about who goes and who stays carries real weight, since the audience will know each character well by the time the decision arrives.

The Team Behind the Camera

Elba co-directs with Martin Owen, who is also credited as the creator of the project. The screenplay is credited to Elizabeth Morris, Matthew Mitchell and Vicki Sargent, and Gina Carter and Matt Williams produce. Mark Silk serves as cinematographer, Ben Mills edits, and Roger Goula composed the score. The production is a collaboration between the United Kingdom and the United States, made by Future Artists Entertainment and distributed by Lionsgate.

The film is reported to be rated R for language and to run 87 minutes, a compact length that fits the real-time pressure the story is built on. For Elba, it is only his second feature as a director, and it continues a long-running pattern of him taking on projects that blend physical intensity with genre storytelling.

A Practical Approach to Production

Elba has said the film was made as practically as possible. The cast learned to hold their breath for several minutes, and a full-size spacecraft was built and submerged for the shoot, per Dark Horizons. Shooting in real water rather than relying entirely on digital effects is a demanding choice, since it places performers in physically stressful conditions and slows every setup. It can also produce a tension that is hard to fake, because the strain on screen is partly genuine.

Elba has also spoken about wanting audiences to travel from space to the ocean with the characters and to feel thrilled and claustrophobic along the way, according to Bloody Disgusting. That emphasis on sensation over spectacle matches the tone of the trailer, which spends more time on faces, breathing and rising water than on large-scale action.

From Market Announcement to Theaters

Lionsgate first announced the project at the American Film Market in 2023, with Elba and Owen attached as co-directors and Elba set to star, according to reports from The Hollywood Reporter and Screen Daily at the time. Roger Goula was later hired to compose the score. The first trailer arrives roughly six weeks before the film reaches cinemas, giving the studio a window to build awareness ahead of the holiday season.

Release Details

Above the Below opens in United States theaters on December 4, 2026, through Lionsgate. In Germany, LEONINE Studios is releasing the film a day earlier, on December 3, 2026, with a German-dubbed trailer already available. Release dates for several other territories, including France and Sweden, had not been announced when the trailer debuted.

The release is theatrical only, and Rotten Tomatoes lists it as a limited release, so availability in the opening weeks may vary by market. Audiences can watch the official trailer above and check local listings closer to the release date.
