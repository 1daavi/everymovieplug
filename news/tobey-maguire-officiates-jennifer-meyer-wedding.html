Tobey Maguire Officiates Ex-Wife Jennifer Meyer's Hawaii Wedding to Geoffrey Ogunlesi

The Spider-Man star presided over the ceremony just weeks after his divorce from Meyer was finalized, according to multiple reports.

Tobey Maguire officiated the wedding of his ex-wife, Jennifer Meyer, and her new husband, Geoffrey Ogunlesi, according to reports from People and Page Six. The ceremony took place on Saturday, October 10, at the Four Seasons Resort Lanai in Hawaii, and the news broke on Sunday when photos from the day surfaced.

TMZ reports that Maguire, 51, wore a suit and tie and read from a piece of paper during the outdoor ceremony. Meyer, a 49-year-old jewelry designer, wore a strapless white gown with a long veil, while Ogunlesi wore a black tuxedo and bow tie.

Page Six reports that the guest list included Leonardo DiCaprio and his girlfriend Vittoria Ceretti, Jeff Bezos and Lauren Sánchez, Courteney Cox, Kate Hudson and her fiancé Danny Fujikawa, and Rachel Zoe. Maguire and Meyer's children, 19-year-old Ruby and 17-year-old Otis, also took part in the weekend's celebrations, and Page Six says Maguire appeared in good spirits while spending time with them.

The wedding came shortly after the end of Maguire and Meyer's marriage. The two wed in 2007 and announced their separation in 2016, and Meyer filed for divorce in 2020. Reports differ on the exact date the divorce became official, with some pointing to late September and others to an early October court filing. Court documents reportedly indicate that some matters tied to the divorce still remain to be settled.

Ogunlesi is the founder and CEO of The Ogunlesi Group and the son of Nigerian billionaire investor Adebayo Ogunlesi. He proposed to Meyer in August 2024, and the couple welcomed their first child together earlier this year.

The news has drawn a wave of reaction online, with many fans praising Maguire for showing up for his ex-wife and her new husband. The post that first spread the story on X alone gathered hundreds of thousands of views within hours.
