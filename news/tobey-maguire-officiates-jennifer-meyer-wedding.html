Tobey Maguire Officiates Ex-Wife Jennifer Meyer's Hawaii Wedding to Geoffrey Ogunlesi

By Maccarthy Albin

October 12, 2026

The Spider-Man star presided over the ceremony just weeks after his divorce from Meyer was finalized, according to multiple reports.

Tobey Maguire officiated the wedding of his ex-wife, Jennifer Meyer, and her new husband, Geoffrey Ogunlesi, according to reports from People and Page Six. The ceremony took place on Saturday, October 10, at the Four Seasons Resort Lanai in Hawaii, and the news broke on Sunday when photos from the day surfaced.

An Outdoor Ceremony on Lanai

TMZ reports that Maguire, 51, wore a suit and tie and read from a piece of paper during the outdoor ceremony. Meyer, a 49-year-old jewelry designer, wore a strapless white gown with a long veil, while Ogunlesi wore a black tuxedo and bow tie.

Lanai is one of the smaller Hawaiian islands, known for its quiet resorts and limited development, which makes it a natural choice for a private celebration. The reports describe an open-air setting rather than a ballroom, and the photographs that circulated on Sunday are what first brought the event to public attention. No official statement from the couple or from Maguire has been cited in the coverage so far.

Who Was on the Guest List

Page Six reports that the guest list included Leonardo DiCaprio and his girlfriend Vittoria Ceretti, Jeff Bezos and Lauren Sánchez, Courteney Cox, Kate Hudson and her fiancé Danny Fujikawa, and Rachel Zoe. Maguire and Meyer’s children, 19-year-old Ruby and 17-year-old Otis, also took part in the weekend’s celebrations, and Page Six says Maguire appeared in good spirits while spending time with them.

The attendees reflect the overlap of Hollywood, fashion and business circles in which Meyer and Maguire have both moved. Meyer is the founder of her own jewelry line and the daughter of longtime entertainment executive Ron Meyer, while Maguire has been a fixture of the film industry since the late 1990s. DiCaprio, in particular, has been a friend of Maguire’s for decades.

The Divorce Timeline

The wedding came shortly after the end of Maguire and Meyer’s marriage. The two wed in 2007 and announced their separation in 2016, and Meyer filed for divorce in 2020. Reports differ on the exact date the divorce became official, with some pointing to late September and others to an early October court filing. Court documents reportedly indicate that some matters tied to the divorce still remain to be settled.

The gap between those dates is notable. The couple were separated for roughly a decade before the legal process concluded, and the reports that the divorce became official only weeks before the new wedding explain why the timing drew attention. Because the sources disagree on the date and the paperwork is described as not fully resolved, readers should treat the precise timeline as unsettled until court records or representatives confirm it.

Meet the Groom

Ogunlesi is the founder and CEO of The Ogunlesi Group and the son of Nigerian billionaire investor Adebayo Ogunlesi. He proposed to Meyer in August 2024, and the couple welcomed their first child together earlier this year.

Adebayo Ogunlesi is a well-known figure in global finance and infrastructure investing, and his family background is why some outlets, such as Newsy Today, describe the groom as a billionaire heir. That label comes from the outlet’s framing rather than from any statement by the family, and the sources used for this report do not give details of Geoffrey Ogunlesi’s own business holdings.

Why an Ex-Husband Officiating Stands Out

Weddings officiated by a former spouse are unusual, and that is a large part of why this story spread so quickly. Celebrity weddings are normally covered for their guest lists and fashion, but the identity of the person presiding is rarely the headline. Here, the presence of Maguire in that role is what turned a private event into a widely shared news item.

The coverage does not quote Maguire or Meyer on why he took on the role, and it would be speculation to assign motives. What the reports do establish is that he was present, took part in the ceremony and, according to Page Six, spent the weekend with his children. For families that have restructured after a divorce, the image of a former couple and their children in the same setting is the part many readers have focused on.

What Remains Unconfirmed

Much of what is known about the day comes from photographs and from outlets citing sources, not from the couple themselves. The image credit on the main photograph is to a photo agency, which indicates the pictures circulated through professional channels. Details such as the length of the ceremony, the vows and the full guest list have not been independently verified, and different outlets have highlighted different attendees.

It is also unclear whether the couple plan to say anything publicly. If representatives for Meyer, Ogunlesi or Maguire issue a statement, it would be the first on-the-record account of the event. Until then, the reporting amounts to a consistent but secondhand picture, and readers should weigh it accordingly.

Maguire’s Place in Hollywood

Maguire is best known for playing Peter Parker in Sam Raimi’s Spider-Man trilogy, which began in 2002 and ran through 2007, and for returning to the role in 2021’s Spider-Man: No Way Home. His earlier work includes The Cider House Rules, Wonder Boys and Pleasantville, and he later appeared in Seabiscuit and The Great Gatsby. The wedding coverage is about his personal life, yet his fame explains the scale of the attention.

The Online Response

The news has drawn a wave of reaction online, with many fans praising Maguire for showing up for his ex-wife and her new husband. The post that first spread the story on X alone gathered hundreds of thousands of views within hours.

Reaction of that kind is typical when a high-profile private event becomes public, and much of it comes from social media users rather than the people involved. Viewership figures for individual posts also change quickly, so the “hundreds of thousands” cited in early coverage is best read as a snapshot of the first hours rather than a final count. As with the divorce timeline, the strongest confirmed facts are the date, the location, the officiant and the guests named in the reports, while interpretations of what the day meant remain opinion.
