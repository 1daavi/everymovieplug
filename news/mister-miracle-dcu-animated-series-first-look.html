James Gunn Unveils Mister Miracle DCU Series With Josh Hutcherson and Mackenzie Davis

The adult animated series is targeting 2028 and has no confirmed streaming home yet.

Mister Miracle, a new adult animated series in James Gunn’s DC Universe, has its leads. Josh Hutcherson voices Scott Free and Mackenzie Davis voices Big Barda, Gunn announced on social media along with a first look.

Tom King, who co-created Lanterns, is showrunner. He adapted his and Mitch Gerads’ comic run and wrote all eight episodes, with Gerads producing, per Tribune. The series is a collaboration between DC Studios and Warner Bros. Animation.

DC Studios co-CEO Peter Safran said the team has always thought of 2028 for the release, and episodes will run about 30 minutes, according to Screen Rant. Screen Rant lists HBO Max as the network, but other outlets say no platform is confirmed.

Nathan Fillion returns as Guy Gardner, and Darkseid is expected to appear. Gunn has said actors cast in major DCU animated projects will also play their live-action counterparts.
