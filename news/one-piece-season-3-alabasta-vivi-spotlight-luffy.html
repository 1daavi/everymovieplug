Netflix’s One Piece Season 3 Shifts the Spotlight From Luffy to Vivi in The Battle of Alabasta

By Maccarthy Albin

October 21, 2026

Luffy is not leaving the show. Screen Rant says the Alabasta story shifts the main focus to Vivi.

Netflix’s live-action One Piece returns for Season 3 in 2027 with a new central focus. Screen Rant reports the season, officially titled One Piece: The Battle of Alabasta, puts Vivi (Charithra Chandran) in the spotlight instead of Luffy.

Luffy is not being written out. Iñaki Godoy returns as Luffy, per Screen Rant. The headline refers to the arc’s focus on Vivi and her story. Netflix has not announced a release date beyond 2027. Season 2 premiered in March 2026, according to TVLine.

There is separate 2027 news for fans. The animated remake The One Piece from WIT Studio premieres on Netflix in February 2027, with seven episodes released at once, covering the first 50 chapters of the manga, per Netflix Tudum.

In Japan, Gamerant reports that One Piece Film: God Valley is set for summer 2027 and is a Luffy-free story built around the God Valley flashback. The long-running Toei anime went on break after episode 1180 on September 27, per FandomWire, and returns in 2027.

How Netflix Announced the Season

Netflix confirmed the title and the 2027 window on April 7, 2026, through a social media post that teased the Alabasta battle as approaching, according to Dark Horizons, which reproduced the streamer’s message. The announcement came shortly after Season 2 arrived, and But Why Tho noted that it represents a much shorter turnaround than the three-year gap between the first and second seasons. Netflix also revealed that the season carries a subtitle this time around, per Tom’s Guide.

The Official Synopsis

Netflix’s synopsis, as quoted by Blavity, says that after agreeing to help Princess Vivi at the end of Season 2, Luffy and the Straw Hat crew face a brewing war in the desert kingdom of Alabasta, Vivi’s homeland. A shorter version of the description, reported by Variety India, says war is coming for Luffy and the crew in Alabasta. In other words, the official description keeps the Straw Hats together as a group, while the Vivi-centered reading comes from Screen Rant’s analysis of the arc.

Picking Up From Season 2

The season continues directly from the second season’s finale and adapts the Alabasta arc from Eiichiro Oda’s manga, according to Respawn. Season 2 premiered in March 2026, per TVLine, and Blavity notes that Chandran joined the series in that season before returning for the third. Cultbox describes the new episodes as a race by the Straw Hats to help Vivi save a homeland on the brink of civil war, which is the same conflict the official synopsis describes.

A Large Returning and Incoming Cast

Godoy, Emily Rudd, Mackenyu, Jacob Romero and Taz Skylar all return as the core crew, with Mikaela Hoover voicing Tony Tony Chopper, according to But Why Tho and Dark Horizons. Chandran returns as Vivi, Joe Manganiello as Crocodile, Lera Abova as Miss All Sunday and Sendhil Ramamurthy as King Nefertari Cobra, per 10and5.

GMA Network reports that the season also cast Xolo Maridueña and Callum Kerr in new roles. Respawn adds that the season introduces Sir Crocodile, Baroque Works and Portgas D. Ace. Tom’s Guide lists further additions including David Dastmalchian as Mr. 3, Camrus Johnson as Mr. 5, Daniel Lasker as Mr. 9 and Mark Harelik as Dr. Hiriluk. MovieWeb describes Vivi as a princess of Alabasta trying to save her country from the ruthless machinations of the warlord Crocodile.

Production and Creative Team

Production took place in Cape Town, South Africa, according to 10and5, which also looked at what the shoot means for the local film industry. What’s on Netflix later reported that filming had wrapped in Cape Town. The series is co-run, written and executive produced by Ian Stokes and Joe Tracz, according to Variety India.

Other One Piece Projects Around the Season

Netflix has paired the season announcement with other Straw Hat content. Anitrendz reports that a two-part animated LEGO special was announced alongside the live-action news, and What’s on Netflix points viewers who cannot wait until 2027 to LEGO One Piece and the animated The One Piece as other Straw Hat content. Together with the Toei anime’s return and the planned God Valley film in Japan, the franchise has a busy schedule through 2027.

What Remains Unannounced

Netflix has not shared an exact premiere date or release schedule for the new season, according to Outlook’s Respawn and Variety India. The release window remains simply 2027.
