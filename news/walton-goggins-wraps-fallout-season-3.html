Walton Goggins Wraps Fallout Season 3 With a Farewell Message From California

The actor says he has taken off the Ghoul makeup for the last time this season, though Prime Video has not confirmed that the full production is finished.

Walton Goggins has finished filming his work on Fallout season 3. The actor, who plays the Ghoul, announced the milestone this week in an Instagram video that doubled as a goodbye to the character’s heavy prosthetics.

In the clip, Goggins is seen driving down a desert highway and mouthing along to Led Zeppelin’s “Ramble On,” according to Screen Rant. His caption began, “Just took it off for the last time,” and went on to say he would miss California “til the next time.” GamesRadar and Deadline both reported on the post.

Is Goggins Leaving the Show?

The wording of the caption briefly worried some fans, and Deadline noted that it had asked Prime Video for comment. Nothing in the post, however, points to Goggins leaving the series. Outlets including Screen Rant and Pixel Twelve read the line as a reference to this season’s schedule, meaning the last day he wore the Ghoul makeup for season 3, rather than a hint about Cooper Howard’s fate.

Goggins’ longtime makeup artist, Jake Garber, also marked the occasion on Instagram, writing that it was a wrap on season 3 for him and calling it “one hell of a season,” per Screen Rant.

Has the Whole Season Finished Shooting?

Not necessarily. Goggins’ post covers his own work, and Prime Video has not announced an official end to production on the full season. The Ghoul is among the show’s central roles, so other cast members and crew are likely close to done, but that remains an assumption.

Timing is broadly consistent with earlier remarks. Kyle MacLachlan, who plays Hank MacLean, said at a book signing in early October that roughly a month of shooting remained, as reported by Screen Rant and GamesRadar. He added that the season could arrive around May or June 2027. That estimate is unofficial, and Prime Video has not announced a premiere date.

How Production Got Here

Cameras began rolling on June 15, 2026, according to Screen Rant. Goggins publicly confirmed his return in July with a photo of the Ghoul makeup process captioned “Back in the saddle,” as covered by Bleeding Cool and SYFY Wire. The production has been based mainly in the Los Angeles area, including Santa Clarita, per Fiction Horizon, and Goggins was spotted shooting in Pomona, California, in August, according to ARY News.

Prime Video ordered the third season in May 2025, months before season 2 premiered in December. Newcomers to the cast include Aaron Paul, Manny Jacinto, Emily Mortimer and Thomasin McKenzie, while Annabel O’Hagan and Dave Register have been promoted to series regular, according to Bleeding Cool. Ella Purnell, Aaron Moten and Goggins return as the central trio.
