2026 Video Game Movies: Release Dates for Resident Evil, Street Fighter, Mortal Kombat II and More

Eight game adaptations were dated for 2026, with Sonic the Hedgehog 4 following in 2027.

2026 has a full slate of video game movies. Per PC Gamer’s tracker, Return to Silent Hill opened January 23, Iron Lung on January 30 and The Mortuary Assistant on February 13.

A Super Mario sequel followed in April. Mortal Kombat II was dated for May 8 after moving from its original October 2025 date, and Zach Cregger’s Resident Evil was set for September 18.

Street Fighter, directed by Kitao Sakurai, was dated for October 16 after moving from March, per MeinMMO. The last film on the list is The Angry Birds Movie 3 from director John Rice on December 23.

Looking ahead, Sonic the Hedgehog 4 is set for March 2027, and PC Gamer reports a Sea of Thieves film is also in the works. Dates for these films have shifted before, so check local listings.
