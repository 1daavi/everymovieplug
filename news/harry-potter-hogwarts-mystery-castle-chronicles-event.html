Harry Potter: Hogwarts Mystery Launches Its Castle Chronicles Event

By Maccarthy Albin

October 15, 2026

The limited-time event sends players into a Defense Against the Dark Arts disaster and reopens old side quests.

The mobile game Harry Potter: Hogwarts Mystery has announced Castle Chronicles, a temporary in-game event revealed on the game's official social channels. Players must prevent a disaster in Defense Against the Dark Arts class.

Trouble in the Classroom

The premise places players in a familiar setting. Defense Against the Dark Arts is one of the best-known classes in the Wizarding World, and in the books and films it is often the setting for dramatic lessons. By building the event around a disaster that must be prevented in that classroom, Castle Chronicles draws on an environment that fans already associate with danger, surprise and memorable teachers.

Beyond that outline, the announcement coverage gathered here does not describe how the event plays out, what challenges it contains or how long each part takes. Those details are likely to become clear once players open the event for themselves.

Old Quests, New Chances

According to Diário da Mídia, the event also reopens Time-Limited Side Quests, with exclusive rewards and items for players who completed the original versions. The story is set before Harry arrives at Hogwarts, so it does not change the main storyline of the books and films. Severus Snape and Albus Dumbledore both appear, and members of the film cast voice characters in the game.

The return of earlier side quests matters to a specific group of players. Time-limited content is, by definition, available for a short period, so those who joined the game after the original versions ended would have had no opportunity to play them. Reopening the quests gives newer players a way to experience that material, while the exclusive rewards for people who finished the originals acknowledge long-term participation.

The decision to set the story before Harry’s arrival also keeps the event separate from the events of the books and films. That gives the writers room to include well-known figures like Snape and Dumbledore without contradicting anything fans already know about the main narrative.

Eight Years of a Mobile Hogwarts

The game launched in 2018 from Jam City and has now been around for eight years. It is free to download on the App Store and Google Play, with in-app purchases. The event appears in the game's events tab, and players need to update the app to access it. Start and end dates have not been published.

A mobile game that remains active for that long is a notable achievement, since many titles in the genre fade within a few years. Regular events are one of the main ways such games keep their communities engaged, because they give returning players a reason to reopen the app and give lapsed players a reason to come back. Castle Chronicles follows that pattern, offering a mix of new story content and returning material.

The Game in Brief

For readers unfamiliar with the title, Hogwarts Mystery is a role-playing game in which players create their own student and move through life at the school, attending classes, meeting characters and following a story that unfolds over time. Because the player character is original rather than one of the main figures from the books, the game can explore corners of the school that the novels and films only touch on. That design is what allows events like Castle Chronicles to place the player in a classroom crisis without needing to involve Harry, Ron or Hermione directly.

What Players Need to Do

For anyone who wants to take part, the steps are straightforward. The first is to update the app through the App Store or Google Play so the new content is available. After that, the event can be found in the events tab inside the game. Because no start or end dates have been announced, players who are interested are better off opening the event sooner rather than waiting, since a limited-time event can close without much notice once its dates are set.

A Franchise Heading Back to Screens

The news arrives ahead of HBO's live-action Harry Potter series, which is set to premiere on December 25, 2026, according to Wikipedia. Screen Rant also reported the Castle Chronicles announcement.

The timing places the game within a wider moment of renewed attention on the franchise. A new television adaptation naturally draws interest back to the stories, and tie-in activity in other formats, including games, tends to benefit from that attention. The sources do not link Castle Chronicles to the HBO series in any official way. The report simply notes that the two have appeared in the news around the same time.

For fans of the games, the more immediate point is the event itself. Castle Chronicles combines a new classroom crisis with returning side quests, familiar characters and the voices of film cast members, all inside a game that has stayed available on phones for eight years. With start and end dates still unannounced, the next details will most likely come through the game’s official social channels, which is where the announcement first appeared.
