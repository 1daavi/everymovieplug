Marvel Studios Posts a Cryptic Binary Message on X Before the VisionQuest Finale: What It Says and What It Means

By Abigail Gyimah

November 10, 2026 · 10:07 PM GMT

The post, written entirely in binary, decodes to a line from the WandaVision finale and lands hours before the VisionQuest season finale streams on Disney+.

Marvel Studios has posted a cryptic message on X, and it is written entirely in binary code. The post appeared on Tuesday, November 10, just hours before the season finale of VisionQuest arrives on Disney+, and when decoded it reads as a line from WandaVision: “We could never truly leave each other, even if we tried,” according to The Direct. The quote has fueled a new round of speculation that Elizabeth Olsen’s Wanda Maximoff is about to appear in the MCU again.

Marvel has not said what the post means and has not confirmed that Olsen is in the finale. What can be established is where the line comes from, how it connects to the series that is ending tonight, and why the timing has prompted so much discussion ahead of Avengers: Doomsday on December 18. This article breaks down each piece.

  • Marvel Studios posted a message in binary code on X on November 10, hours before the VisionQuest finale
  • The decoded text is a line Wanda says to her sons in the WandaVision series finale
  • The finale, titled “Forever,” streams on Disney+ at 6 p.m. PT / 9 p.m. ET on Tuesday
  • Marvel has promised “one final surprise,” but has not confirmed whether Wanda appears
  • VisionQuest is the last Marvel release before Avengers: Doomsday, which opens December 18

The post itself

The official Marvel Studios account shared the message on X with no caption or explanation. The post is embedded below so readers can see exactly what Marvel published.

View the full post on X

Binary messages of this kind store text as strings of zeros and ones, usually in groups of eight digits, with each group standing for a single character. In the common ASCII system, for example, the group 01010111 represents a capital W. Once the groups are converted one by one, they spell out a sentence, which is how fans decoded the post within minutes of it going live.

The line comes from the WandaVision finale

The decoded sentence is a quote from “The Series Finale,” the ninth and last episode of WandaVision, which streamed on Disney+ on March 5, 2021. The episode was directed by Matt Shakman and written by Jac Schaeffer. In the scene, Wanda tucks her sons, Billy and Tommy, into bed before she takes down the Hex that she placed over Westview, New Jersey. She tells them that a family is forever and that they could never truly leave each other, even if they tried.

That was the last time the Maximoff family was seen together. The boys, who were created by Wanda’s magic, faded out of existence when the Hex came down, and Wanda left Westview alone. The moment has remained one of the emotional high points of the MCU’s television output, and it is the reason a message built from those words carries so much weight for viewers who have followed the story from the beginning.

Why Marvel posted it now

The post arrived on the day of the final episode of VisionQuest, the series that concludes what Marvel describes as the WandaVision trilogy, which also includes Agatha All Along. According to The Direct, Marvel Studios president Kevin Feige has called it Wanda’s trilogy even though she appears in only one of its three installments. VisionQuest follows Paul Bettany’s Vision, the rebuilt White Vision who escaped the WandaVision finale, and it also features James Spader as Ultron.

The series premiered on October 13 with two episodes and then arrived in double drops over the next two weeks, followed by single episodes on November 3 and November 10. Episode 8, titled “Forever,” streams on Tuesday at 6 p.m. PT, which is 9 p.m. ET. Showrunner Terry Matalas wrote and directed the finale, according to reporting on the release schedule. The title itself echoes the line in Marvel’s post, and the penultimate episode, “Immolation,” included a reference to the same idea when Vision gave Tommy episodes of WandaVision to watch.

Going into the finale, 9to5Marvel reports that the series holds a 90 percent score on Rotten Tomatoes, and it lists the finale as running about an hour and eight minutes, longer than the earlier episodes. The seventh episode ended on a cliffhanger that finally introduced the mysterious Client, the figure behind much of the season’s conflict, so the final chapter has plot threads to resolve alongside any emotional payoff. Once the finale streams, all eight episodes of the first season will be available on Disney+.

The trail of earlier teases

The binary message is the latest in a run of promotional posts that point at Wanda. On November 5, Marvel Studios released a trailer for the finale with the tagline “One Vision. One moment. One final surprise.” In the days since, the official WandaVision Instagram account has shared a string of coded posts, including one with the question “Who have you lost?” and another with the cut-off words “Billy… Tomm…” The first line comes from Vision’s scene with the artificial intelligence J.I.M. in episode seven, when Vision is forced to admit that his greatest fear is the loss of Billy, Tommy and Wanda.

Marvel also reminded fans of Wanda’s goodbye to her sons in August, when the official account shared the same clip. Several VisionQuest promos have featured old footage of Wanda, and the series itself is built around a character made of code, a detail that makes a binary post a fitting choice for the final push.

Cryptic social posts are a familiar part of Marvel’s marketing. Before the finale of X-Men ’97 in 2024, the studio posted an image of Jubilee’s pink cell phone with a working phone number that fans could call. In September 2026, Marvel Studios Japan used ASCII art inspired by the dust of Thanos’ snap to promote the Avengers: Endgame Encore screenings. Each campaign gave fans a puzzle to solve before the release it was promoting.

What fans think the message means

The strongest theory is that the finale will bring Wanda back. SuperHeroHype reported that fans are convinced the post points toward a return of the Scarlet Witch, who is considered dead after the events of Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness in 2022. Olsen is not listed as a cast member of VisionQuest, and Marvel has not confirmed that she will return. After seven episodes, Wanda has not appeared in the series.

A second theory focuses on her sons. The finale trailer showed Tommy walking into a candle-lit room covered in runes, as well as a hand wrapped in blue magic with black nail polish. Many viewers believe the hand belongs to Joe Locke’s Billy Maximoff, who used the same signature magic in Agatha All Along. Locke added to the talk in September when he wrote, “My brother has his own Marvel show !!!” on social media. That would make the “one final surprise” a reunion of the Maximoff family, with Wanda present through memory even if she is not there in person.

Some of the speculation comes from people who worked on the show. VisionQuest editor Drew Nichols replied to a Marvel post about the “one final surprise” by writing that there might be more than one surprise. Fans took the comment as a hint that Billy could be only part of what is coming. Others note that Wanda was buried under the temple at Mount Wundagore at the end of Multiverse of Madness, but that no body was shown, which leaves room for a return. None of these readings has been confirmed.

Collider framed the finale as the last opportunity to address what it called the MCU’s biggest loose end, the unresolved question of what happened to Wanda after Multiverse of Madness. The same article noted that Bettany has described the show as driven by Vision’s attempt to rediscover his emotions, comments he made at a New York Comic Con panel. Together those points explain why a family-centered quote, rather than a plot clue, was chosen for the final teaser: the series has treated Vision’s search for his past, and for the people he lost, as its emotional core.

The Avengers: Doomsday connection

The timing matters because VisionQuest is the last Marvel release before Avengers: Doomsday, according to Collider, which makes the finale the final chance for the MCU to address questions it has left open about Wanda’s fate. The film, directed by Anthony and Joe Russo and starring Robert Downey Jr. as Doctor Doom, opens on December 18, 2026. Rumors have also linked Olsen to the film through a reported Latverian witch costume, a claim The Direct mentioned and that Marvel has not addressed.

What to watch for next

The finale answers the most direct question first: whether the “one final surprise” is Billy, Wanda or something else. After it streams, attention will turn to whether Marvel confirms any link between VisionQuest and Avengers: Doomsday, and whether the studio continues its pattern of coded posts as the December release approaches. Until Marvel confirms details, the binary message remains a teaser and not an announcement.
