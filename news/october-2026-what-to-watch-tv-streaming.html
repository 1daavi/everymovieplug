What to Watch in October: Streaming and TV Highlights

By Maccarthy Albin

October 10, 2026

A guide to the biggest premieres still to come this month.

October is packed with fall premieres and seasonal horror. These are some of the dates worth marking, based on the lineups published by streaming and entertainment outlets.

Coming Up

  • October 7: Carrie premieres on Prime Video.
  • October 10: 100 Days of Deception arrives on Netflix as part of its Asian lineup.
  • October 15: The Diplomat Season 4 premieres on Netflix. Guy Ritchie's In the Grey, about a covert team recovering a stolen fortune, also arrives.
  • October 16: KATSEYE: WILD HEARTS premieres on Disney+.
  • October 22: Nobody Wants This returns to Netflix.

Coven Academy premiered on Disney+ on October 2, and the Prime Video horror slate continues through the month.

Streaming dates can change, so check each service before planning a watch night.

Carrie Brings Stephen King to Prime Video

The month's biggest horror event is Carrie, an eight-episode series that Prime Video released in full on October 7. Mike Flanagan, the creator behind The Haunting of Hill House and Midnight Mass, serves as showrunner and writer and directs four episodes, with Stephen King listed as an executive producer. It is the first long-form television adaptation of King's 1974 debut novel, and it updates the story with modern pressures such as social media bullying.

Summer H. Howell plays Carrie White after a casting search that considered nearly 1,500 actors, and Samantha Sloyan appears as her mother, Margaret. The ensemble also includes Siena Agudong, Amber Midthunder, Matthew Lillard and Heather Graham, along with several Flanagan regulars such as Kate Siegel and Rahul Kohli. The project is also Flanagan's first for Prime Video since he left Netflix.

A Period Spy Drama From Korea

100 Days of Deception launched on October 10 and follows a weekly schedule rather than a single release. The 16-episode series from director Yoo In-shik, whose credits include Extraordinary Attorney Woo, adds two new episodes every Saturday and Sunday and runs until its finale at the end of November. Set in Gyeongseong in 1932 under Japanese colonial rule, it stars Kim You-jung as Lee Ga-kyung, a pickpocket who is sent undercover as a translator inside the colonial government. Park Jin-young plays the other lead, and the story blends espionage, romance and period action.

Netflix's Big Mid-October Pair

October 15 is the most crowded day on the calendar. The Diplomat returns for a fourth season with all eight episodes available at once. Keri Russell and Rufus Sewell lead as Kate and Hal Wyler, and Allison Janney and Bradley Whitford step up to series regular status as the president and her husband. The season was shot in London, Washington, New York and Lucca, Italy. According to the official description, two marriages collide, and Kate and Todd must join forces to stop an alliance between Grace and Hal from upsetting the balance of power at home and abroad.

The same day brings Guy Ritchie's In the Grey, a heist thriller that opened in theaters in May. Jake Gyllenhaal and Henry Cavill play Bronco and Sid, a pair of operatives sent to recover a billion-dollar fortune from a criminal organization, with Eiza González and Rosamund Pike in the supporting cast. The film runs just under 100 minutes, which makes it an easy choice for a single evening.

A Music Documentary for K-Pop Fans

On October 16, KATSEYE: WILD HEARTS reaches Disney+ and, in the United States, Hulu. Directed by Nadia Hallgren, who also made the Netflix series Pop Star Academy: KATSEYE, the roughly 80-minute film picks up after the group's debut and shows rehearsals, studio sessions and interviews alongside videos submitted by fans. It had a limited theatrical run in August before moving to streaming.

Nobody Wants This Returns for Season 3

The month closes on a lighter note with the return of Nobody Wants This on October 22. Netflix renewed the romantic comedy less than two weeks after its second season premiered, when that season led the platform's most-watched list. Kristen Bell and Adam Brody return as Joanne and Noah, who are now moving into a home together. Justine Lupe and Timothy Simons also return as Morgan and Sasha, and creator Erin Foster has a guest role. The new season is expected to feel lighter than the more turbulent second run.

Planning Your Watch List

The schedule is easy to split by mood. Horror fans have Carrie early in the month, drama viewers can settle into the long-running political plots of The Diplomat, and anyone who prefers a weekly rhythm can follow 100 Days of Deception across the autumn weekends. Families and music fans have KATSEYE: WILD HEARTS, while Nobody Wants This offers a comfortable finish to the month.
