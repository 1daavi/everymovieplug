What to Watch in October: Streaming and TV Highlights

A guide to the biggest premieres still to come this month.

October is packed with fall premieres and seasonal horror. These are some of the dates worth marking, based on the lineups published by streaming and entertainment outlets.

Coming Up

  • October 7: Carrie premieres on Prime Video.
  • October 10: 100 Days of Deception arrives on Netflix as part of its Asian lineup.
  • October 15: The Diplomat Season 4 premieres on Netflix. Guy Ritchie's In the Grey, about a covert team recovering a stolen fortune, also arrives.
  • October 16: KATSEYE: WILD HEARTS premieres on Disney+.
  • October 22: Nobody Wants This returns to Netflix.

Coven Academy premiered on Disney+ on October 2, and the Prime Video horror slate continues through the month.

Streaming dates can change, so check each service before planning a watch night.
