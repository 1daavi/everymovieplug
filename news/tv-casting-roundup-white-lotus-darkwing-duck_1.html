TV Casting Roundup: The White Lotus, Darkwing Duck and More

By Maccarthy Albin

October 10, 2026

A quick look at the latest casting moves across TV.

Here are the latest casting and development stories from TVLine.

  • The White Lotus Season 4: Helena Bonham Carter has dropped out of the cast.
  • Katie Greaves (Fox): Four-time Emmy winner Edie Falco will play the title role in the network's new legal drama.
  • Darkwing Duck (Disney+): The animated character is getting a revival at Disney+.
  • Paradise Season 3: Julianna Margulies has joined the cast in a recurring role.
  • The Morning Show: The series has added a Will Trent veteran to its cast.

Further details on release dates and character roles have not been announced for most of these projects.

The White Lotus loses Helena Bonham Carter

The exit was first reported in the spring. Bonham Carter left roughly a week after filming began, and HBO said the character Mike White created for her didn't work once cameras rolled. The role is being rewritten and recast. Season 4 is set on the French Riviera during the Cannes Film Festival and is shooting in Cannes, Saint-Tropez and Monaco, with some work in Paris. White writes and directs every episode. Chris Messina, Steve Coogan, Kumail Nanjiani, Sandra Bernhard, Heather Graham and Max Greenfield remain in the cast.

Edie Falco leads Fox's Katie Greaves

Fox gave the show a series order in September. Falco plays Katie Greaves, a former New Hampshire public defender who leaves her career after a family tragedy, then gets pulled back into casework while raising her two young granddaughters. Sharr White is showrunner, and Ridley Scott's Scott Free Productions is an executive producer. Falco is also an executive producer. She is best known for The Sopranos and Nurse Jackie, and no premiere date has been announced.

Darkwing Duck returns with a star voice cast

The new Disney+ series revives the 1991 cartoon, which ran three seasons and 91 episodes. Mark Hamill voices Drake Mallard, the masked crimefighter, with Seth Rogen as Launchpad McQuack, Lizzy Caplan as Gosalyn Mallard, Lilimar as Duckie and Dan Stevens as Glade McTowers. The official synopsis has a rebellious teen named Duckie who knows all about the hero's past and pulls him back into battle against old enemies including Negaduck and Megavolt.

Julianna Margulies joins Paradise

Margulies is cast in a recurring role in Season 3 of Hulu's thriller, created by Dan Fogelman and starring Sterling K. Brown. Her character has not been revealed. She is the only new cast member announced so far, and Season 3 is in production and expected in early 2027. She is known for ER, The Good Wife and The Morning Show.

The Morning Show adds a Will Trent alum

The Will Trent veteran is Sydney Park, who played the younger version of Sonja Sohn's Amanda Wagner. In Season 5 she recurs as Leah, the new assistant to Billy Crudup's Cory Ellison. TVLine has reported that the Apple TV series will end with this season.
