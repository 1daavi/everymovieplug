Little House on the Prairie Wraps Season 2 and Adds 10 Cast Members

The second season adapts On the Banks of Plum Creek.

Filming has wrapped on Season 2 of Netflix's Little House on the Prairie, and ten new actors have joined the cast, according to What's on Netflix. Production had been expected to run into November, so it finished a few weeks early.

Showrunner Rebecca Sonnenshine thanked the crew and the production partners CBS Studios, Anonymous Content and Netflix. The season adapts Laura Ingalls Wilder's On the Banks of Plum Creek.

The New Cast

  • David Wronko as Thomas Perry (recurring)
  • Erik Athavale as Reverend Alden (recurring)
  • Elyse Levesque as Emma Perry (recurring)
  • Graham Harvey as Johnny Johnson (recurring)
  • Jacinta Forest Lewitski as Hannah Walker (recurring)
  • Lyla Porter-Follows as Mrs. Lena Nelson (recurring)
  • Martin Roach as Simon Hughes (recurring)
  • Paul Greene as Luther Perry (recurring)
  • Ryan Northcott as William Oleson (recurring)
  • Scott Porter as Peter Ingalls (guest star)

Willa Dunn (Nellie Oleson), Charlotte Sullivan (Margaret Oleson) and Rachelle Lefevre (Eva Beadle) were announced earlier. Returning from Season 1 are Alice Halsey as Laura Ingalls, Luke Bracey as Charles Ingalls, Crosby Fitzgerald as Caroline Ingalls, Skywalker Hughes as Mary Ingalls and Warren Christie as John Edwards.

When Is It Coming?

Netflix has not announced a premiere date. What's on Netflix estimates a summer 2027 release, but that is its own projection, not an official date. Based on the episode listings, the season appears to run eight episodes.
