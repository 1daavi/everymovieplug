HBO’s Harry Potter Budget: Filings Point to About $25 Million an Episode and Over $200 Million for Season 1

UK accounts show the first season had already cost £147.4 million by the end of 2025, with filming still ongoing.

HBO’s Harry Potter series is on course to rank among the most expensive shows the network has made, according to accounts filed in the UK. The first season could average about £18.8 million ($25.2 million) per episode, Deadline reports, and TheWrap, in a report published October 23, puts the investment in season 1 at more than $200 million.

The figures come from Plum Jam Productions, the HBO-owned company producing the show in the UK. Its filings with Companies House, first reported by The Times, show spending of £147.4 million ($197 million) by the end of 2025. Filming began in July 2025 and did not wrap until May 2026, so the final cost for season one will be higher. Winter Is Coming, citing The Times, notes that the end of 2025 was roughly halfway through production, and projects a final budget above $250 million, or about $31.25 million per episode across eight episodes.

Deadline calculated its per-episode estimate from the company’s reported 2025 revenue of £130.6 million ($175 million). That would put the series in the same bracket as the final season of Game of Thrones, widely reported at $15 million an episode, and The Last of Us. Another report put the season at as much as £150 million ($201 million). TheWrap puts the initial setup at about $200 million (roughly £150 million), says the figure is derived from earnings filings and may not capture full production costs, and reports that episodic budgets are on par with those of House of the Dragon and The Last of Us. Its per-episode estimates are about $15 million for the final season of Game of Thrones, $15 million to $20 million for House of the Dragon and $10 million to $15 million for The Last of Us.

A source told Deadline that costs are front-loaded because of the large sets built at Warner Bros. Studios Leavesden, which means average budgets for later seasons are likely to drop. For comparison, the most expensive of the original films, Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince, reportedly cost $250 million, and the eight films together cost roughly $1.2 billion. TheWrap notes the first Harry Potter film reportedly had a $125 million budget. A final season-one cost above $250 million, as Winter Is Coming projects, would surpass the Half-Blood Prince figure.

HBO has not confirmed a budget. The $100 million per episode figure that has circulated online does not match the filings, which work out to roughly $24.5 million an episode if all spending to date went to season one’s eight episodes. Harry Potter and the Philosopher’s Stone premieres on HBO and HBO Max on December 25, and season two is already in production. The series is planned for seven seasons, each adapting one book, and only two seasons are officially greenlit.
