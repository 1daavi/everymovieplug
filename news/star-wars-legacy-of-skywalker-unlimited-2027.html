Star Wars: Unlimited Confirms Legacy of Skywalker Set for 2027

The booster set covers the Skywalker family across every era of the franchise, but Fantasy Flight has not given an exact release date.

Fantasy Flight Games has confirmed Legacy of Skywalker as an upcoming set for its trading card game Star Wars: Unlimited, with a release in 2027. Screen Rant reports the set is officially confirmed for next year, but no exact release date has been announced.

Legacy of Skywalker is a standard booster set that covers the Skywalker family across every era of the Star Wars saga, according to eGamers. Specific card details are still scarce.

Fantasy Flight revealed the 2027 roadmap during its Galactic Report broadcast at the Star Wars: Unlimited Galactic Championship in Las Vegas, held July 24 to 26, according to W. Eric Martin. Besides Legacy of Skywalker, the roadmap lists Icons 2027 Edition, System Overload, Icons 2028 Edition and Galaxy at War, all through the end of 2027 and none with a release date.

The next set to arrive is Homeworlds, which is due in October 2026. ICv2 reports it is a 260-card booster set centered on forces attacking and defending homeworlds across the galaxy, with Chewbacca, Ezra Bridger, C-3PO, Grand Moff Tarkin and Director Krennic among the featured characters.
