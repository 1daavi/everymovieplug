Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 Is Now on Netflix as Cyberpunk 2077 Update 2.5 Arrives October 22

The standalone sequel follows a new crew in Night City, with Clancy Brown voicing Weak.

Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 is streaming on Netflix. All 10 episodes arrived on Tuesday, October 20 at 12:00 a.m. PT, according to Primetimer and GamesRadar.

The series is a standalone story set in 2078, after the first season ended with David's death. It follows Weak, a former edgerunner legend living without chrome, and D, a netrunner seeking revenge who uncovers corporate secrets, per Primetimer. CD Projekt Red co-CEO Michal Nowakowski has said it is not a direct sequel.

Clancy Brown voices Weak in English, with Nazeeh Tarsha as D, Kimoy Lee as Talia and Valeria Rodriguez as Roman Carax. Hideo Kojima cameos as Hideyoshi Oshima. Studio TRIGGER produces with CD Projekt Red, and Kai Ikarashi directs. The Cure performs the opening theme.

GamesRadar says early reactions from the premiere screening were very positive.

In the game, Cyberpunk 2077 update 2.5 arrives October 22 with Edgerunners 2 content, a new dual-wielding playstyle and cyberware customization, according to GamesRadar. A REDstreams episode on October 21 at 11 a.m. ET will share more details.
