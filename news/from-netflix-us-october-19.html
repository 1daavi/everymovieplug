Horror Series From Heads to Netflix US on October 19

By Maccarthy Albin

October 14, 2026

The first three seasons arrive for a limited run while the final season is still filming for MGM+.

The sci-fi horror series From is coming to Netflix in the United States on Monday, October 19, according to What's On Netflix. The first three seasons will be available at launch, as part of a limited-time licensing window.

A Town Nobody Can Leave

The series, created by John Griffin, follows the residents of a small town in middle America that traps everyone who enters. Escape appears impossible, and terrifying creatures come out of the forest at night. Harold Perrineau stars as Boyd Stevens, the town's de facto sheriff, alongside Catalina Sandino Moreno, Eion Bailey, Scott McCord, Hannah Cheramy, Simon Webster, Ricky He and Elizabeth Saunders. Jeff Pinkner and Jack Bender, both known for Lost, are executive producers.

The premise combines two kinds of fear. One is the supernatural threat that arrives after dark, and the other is the strain of living in a community that cannot get out and must decide how to organize itself. That mix gives the series room for both suspense and character drama. The involvement of two executive producers from Lost is a natural point of comparison for viewers, since that earlier series also centered on a group of people stranded in a place with unexplained rules, though From is its own story with a different tone and structure.

Why It Matters That the Series Is Moving Platforms

From premiered on Epix, now MGM+, in 2022 and has become the network’s most-watched series. Season 4 debuted on MGM+ in April 2026. Season 5 will be the last: production began in Halifax, Nova Scotia on July 6, 2026, is scheduled to wrap on November 27, and the season is set to premiere on MGM+ in 2027.

A series on a smaller premium service can build a loyal audience while remaining unfamiliar to people who do not subscribe to that service. A listing on Netflix puts the show in front of a much larger group of viewers, many of whom may never have seen it. That is why a limited licensing window carries weight: it works as an invitation to catch up before the story concludes. With the final season still in production, the timing allows new viewers to watch three seasons before the last chapter arrives on MGM+ in 2027.

The Production Timeline

  • 2022: premiere on Epix, now MGM+
  • April 2026: Season 4 debuts on MGM+
  • July 6, 2026: Season 5 production begins in Halifax, Nova Scotia
  • November 27, 2026: scheduled end of production
  • 2027: Season 5 premieres on MGM+

The Cast and Creative Team

Perrineau leads the ensemble as Boyd Stevens, the town’s de facto sheriff, a role that places him at the center of the community’s attempts to keep order. He is also familiar to viewers of Lost, which adds a further link between the two series. The wider cast, including Moreno, Bailey, McCord, Cheramy, Webster, He and Saunders, portrays residents and visitors whose stories intersect across the seasons. Griffin’s role as creator and the experience of Pinkner and Bender as executive producers give the show a creative leadership with a track record in serialized mystery storytelling.

A Short-Term Deal, Not a Permanent Home

The report describes the Netflix deal as a promotional move by Amazon MGM Studios ahead of the final season, and notes that the studio’s recent Netflix licenses have usually been short-term. A 2024 international deal licensed only the first season in at least nine countries, and those listings have since been removed, so viewers in the US should not assume the show will stay for long.

The history of the earlier international arrangement is the most concrete evidence of how these deals work. Only the first season was licensed, and the listings later disappeared. The US arrangement is broader in that it includes three seasons from the start, but it is described in the same way, as a window of limited duration. For anyone planning to watch, the practical advice from the report is to begin soon and not to assume the series will remain on the service indefinitely.

What the report does not state is an end date for the US licensing window. That information has not been published, so the length of availability remains unknown.

What Viewers Can Expect Next

As described in the report, the launch lineup covers the first three seasons. Viewers who finish those episodes would need to turn to MGM+ for Season 4, which debuted there in April 2026, and for the final season when it premieres. The production schedule gives a rough picture of the next year: filming is expected to continue through the end of November, and the finale season is set to arrive sometime in 2027, though no specific premiere date has been announced.

Until more details arrive, the confirmed picture is straightforward: the series reaches Netflix in the US on October 19, the first three seasons are included, the arrangement is temporary, and the story will conclude with a fifth season on MGM+ in 2027.
