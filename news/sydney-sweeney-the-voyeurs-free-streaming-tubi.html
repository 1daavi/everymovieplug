Sydney Sweeney’s R-Rated Erotic Thriller The Voyeurs Is Now Free to Stream on Tubi

By Maccarthy Albin

October 19, 2026

The 2021 thriller joined Tubi’s free, ad-supported library on October 1.

Sydney Sweeney’s 2021 erotic thriller The Voyeurs is streaming for free on Tubi in the US, according to Screen Rant. The film arrived on the ad-supported service on October 1 and is still on Prime Video for subscribers.

Where to Watch and What It Costs

For viewers in the United States, the film now has two streaming homes. Tubi offers it at no charge, supported by commercials, while Prime Video continues to carry it for subscribers to that service. The practical difference is straightforward: those who do not want another monthly subscription can watch it on Tubi with advertising breaks, and those who already pay for Prime Video can watch it there.

The film carries an R rating and is intended for adult audiences, a point that is worth keeping in mind for a free service that is easy to open on any connected device.

The Setup

Sweeney plays Pippa, an optometrist in training who moves into a new apartment with her boyfriend Thomas (Justice Smith). The couple discover they can see into an apartment across the street and become entangled in the neighbors’ lives, with dangerous results. Ben Hardy and Natasha Liu Bordizzo co-star.

The idea of two people watching a neighbor through a window has a long history in thrillers, and the premise recalls the kind of suspense stories that Alfred Hitchcock made famous. The film treats that setup as a way to examine what happens when curiosity turns into obsession, with the couple’s relationship under strain as their interest in the people across the street grows.

Behind the Camera and the Critics

Michael Mohan wrote and directed the film and later directed Sweeney in Immaculate (2024). MovieWeb notes the story borrows from a Hitchcock classic. The Voyeurs holds 45 percent on Rotten Tomatoes, though Men’s Journal notes RogerEbert.com gave it three stars out of four.

Taken together, those two scores show a film that divided reviewers. A 45 percent rating on Rotten Tomatoes places it on the negative side of the aggregate, while a three-star review from a respected critical outlet shows that some writers found real merit in it. The creative partnership between Mohan and Sweeney also continued beyond this film, since he directed her again in Immaculate three years later.

Why Tubi Titles Come and Go

Tubi is owned by Fox, and its catalog varies by country. The film also streamed there in May, and MovieWeb reports Tubi usually licenses movies on one-month terms, so availability may change.

That licensing pattern explains why a film can appear on a free service, leave and then return a few months later, as this one has. Titles are rented for short periods, and when the agreement ends the film is removed unless the license is renewed. Viewers who want to watch it should treat the October window as temporary, and since the catalog also differs from one country to another, those outside the US should check their own region’s listings before assuming the film is available.

A Quick Checklist for Viewers

  • Confirm you are in the United States, where the Tubi listing was reported.
  • Expect advertisements on the free service.
  • Remember that the film is also on Prime Video for subscribers.
  • Do not assume the film will remain available beyond the current licensing period.

An Early Lead Role in Context

When The Voyeurs was released in 2021, Sweeney was already known to television audiences for her work on Euphoria and The White Lotus, and the film gave her a lead role in a feature built around a single central character. Her later film work, including the 2023 romantic comedy Anyone but You and the 2024 horror film Immaculate, widened the range of parts associated with her name. Looking at The Voyeurs alongside those projects shows how her career has moved between genres, and why a film from earlier in that path draws renewed interest when it becomes easy to watch.

Sweeney’s Recent Thriller Run

Sweeney’s more recent thriller The Housemaid reached No. 1 on the Apple TV Store after its February digital release, and a sequel, The Housemaid’s Secret, has been confirmed.

The strong digital performance of that film is part of why her earlier thrillers continue to attract attention. When a performer scores a notable success, older titles in the same genre often draw new viewers, and a free listing on Tubi lowers the barrier for anyone who wants to explore her earlier work. The confirmed sequel to The Housemaid keeps her name connected to the genre, though the sources used here do not give further information on when it will arrive or what its story will cover.

For now, the verified facts are limited and clear. The film is on Tubi in the US since October 1, it remains on Prime Video, its critical reception is mixed, and its stay on the free service may be short. Anyone interested in watching it for free has the best chance of doing so while the current listing is active.
