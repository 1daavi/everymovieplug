Michael Cast and Filmmakers to Receive the Gotham Musical Tribute

By Maccarthy Albin

October 16, 2026

The Michael Jackson biopic will be honored at the 36th Annual Gotham Film Awards on November 30.

The cast and creative team behind Michael will receive the Gotham Musical Tribute at the 2026 Gotham Film Awards. The Gotham Film & Media Institute announced the honor on Friday, October 16, according to Next Best Picture and Variety.

The 36th Annual Gotham Film Awards takes place Monday, November 30, at Cipriani Wall Street in New York City, per Next Best Picture. Gotham Film & Media Institute Executive Director Jeffrey Sharp said of the Michael team: “Their extraordinary work captures the humanity behind one of the world's most celebrated artists.”

The other tributes announced are LAIKA for the Gotham Anniversary Tribute, Danny Boyle for the Gotham Director Tribute for Ink, and La Bola Negra for the Gotham Vanguard Tribute. Nominees will be announced on October 27.

The Lionsgate film passed $1 billion worldwide in July, according to Celluloid Junkie, making it the highest-grossing biopic and musical biopic of all time and surpassing Bohemian Rhapsody. It is Lionsgate's first $1 billion film. Lionsgate handled the domestic release, and Universal Pictures International distributed it in most other international markets.
